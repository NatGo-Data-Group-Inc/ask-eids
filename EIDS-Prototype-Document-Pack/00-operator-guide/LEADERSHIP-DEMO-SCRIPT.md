# Leadership Demo Script

## Opening
“Everything you are about to see is synthetic but operationally realistic. We built the corpus so the application can demonstrate how fragmented program knowledge becomes a coherent, queryable product record.”

## Step 1 — Portfolio landing
- Start with only Wave 00 ingested.
- Show that Optima looks healthy, Dental looks useful but incomplete, and ESSENCE looks continuity-poor.
- Make the point that the application is already surfacing not just status, but documentation quality.

## Step 2 — Open Dental
- Show Knowledge Health.
- Explain that the product has baseline docs, structured exports, one transcript, and limited emails.
- Ask: “Who are the key stakeholders?”
- Ask: “What are the main risks right now?”

## Step 3 — Ingest Wave 01
- Refresh the product.
- Show new timeline density and stronger Ask evidence.
- Ask: “What decisions were made this sprint?”
- Point out that the answer is now stronger because the sprint review transcript and approval email exist.

## Step 4 — Ingest Wave 02
- Refresh the product again.
- Show the product at its worst point.
- Ask: “Why is Dental at risk?”
- Open Data and show blockers and action items.
- Generate a weekly report and show that it now tells a coherent escalation story.

## Step 5 — Ingest Wave 03
- Refresh once more.
- Ask: “What changed since baseline?”
- Generate the final report.
- Open the final leadership readout deck in Sources / Attachments.

## Closing line
“The point of the application is not that AI writes prettier summaries. The point is that, as evidence arrives, the system preserves continuity, explains causality, and tells leadership exactly how confident it should be in the answer.”
