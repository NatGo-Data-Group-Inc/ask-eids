# Ingestion Order

Use the pack in this exact order if you want the cleanest leadership demo.

## Wave 00 — Baseline
Upload everything under `wave-00-baseline/`.

### What should happen
- Dental appears useful but incomplete.
- Optima appears healthy and well documented.
- ESSENCE appears caution / continuity-poor.
- Ask answers for Dental should be decent, but not highly confident.
- Reports should contain coverage or completeness warnings.

## Wave 01 — Operational Week
Upload everything under `wave-01-operational/`.

### What should happen
- Dental timeline gets denser.
- A stakeholder approval email and a decision-rich sprint review transcript arrive.
- FHIR dependency becomes explicit and explainable.
- Ask answers should become more specific and cite stronger evidence.

## Wave 02 — Escalation Week
Upload everything under `wave-02-escalation/`.

### What should happen
- Dental status should look materially worse but better explained.
- Blockers become active rather than implied.
- Leadership decision logic becomes visible in transcripts and structured action items.
- Generated reports should tell a coherent “why we are at risk” story.

## Wave 03 — Recovery Week
Upload everything under `wave-03-recovery/`.

### What should happen
- Dental should improve from severe risk to managed caution.
- Ask should be able to answer before/after questions well.
- Reports should explain why confidence improved without pretending the risk is gone.
- The final leadership deck should make the app feel like a true program knowledge system, not just file storage.
