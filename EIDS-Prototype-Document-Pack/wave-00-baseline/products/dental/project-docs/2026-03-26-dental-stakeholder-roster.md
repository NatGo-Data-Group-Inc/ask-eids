# Stakeholder Summary

The Dental / DenClass modernization effort has a mixed stakeholder model that spans delivery ownership, customer acceptance, vendor coordination, and portfolio reporting. This roster is intended to give the knowledge hub a clean answer to the question, "Who matters for this product and why?"

## Primary Stakeholders

### Dr. Minji Sohl — Clinical stakeholder / acceptance authority
- Reviews test cadence and determines whether the demonstration path is credible.
- Expects explicit distinction between approved schedule and proven readiness.
- Sensitive to insufficient integration-test evidence.

### Juan Rivera — Operations / cost and readiness reviewer
- Requests concise impact statements when schedule changes affect release posture.
- Primarily interested in cost, assumptions, and implications to PI and release commitments.

### Lowry Beckett — Vendor and dependency lead
- Owns the external dependency picture.
- Tracks sandbox readiness, named-user credentialing, and vendor milestone commitments.
- Frequently appears in risk, blocker, and action-item artifacts.

### Benjiman Jennings — Portfolio decision authority
- Decides when status posture should change.
- Wants product reporting grounded in evidence rather than verbal context.
- Expects clean narrative alignment between weekly updates, risk logs, and meeting decisions.

## Product Line Leads

### Robin Holt
- Reviews cross-product continuity and portfolio reporting discipline.
- Uses weeklys and generated reports to prepare higher-level briefings.

### Chris Alvarez
- Consumes the same narrative from a delivery and product-line readiness perspective.
- Focused on what changed, what was deferred, and what decision comes next.

## Working Assumptions
- Stakeholders want fewer ad hoc status conversations and more direct access to evidence-backed answers.
- The product manager remains the narrative owner, but the application should preserve continuity if ownership changes.
- Risk and blocker posture should always reference explicit owners and next decision dates.
