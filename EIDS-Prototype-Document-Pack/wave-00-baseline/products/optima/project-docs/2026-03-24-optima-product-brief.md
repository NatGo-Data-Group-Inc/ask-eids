# Optima Product Brief

Optima Care Coordination is included in this demo pack as the healthy comparison case.

## Why Optima matters in the demo
Leadership should be able to compare Dental and Essence against a product that has:
- current weekly updates,
- a clean decision trail,
- a current transcript,
- low-noise stakeholder communications,
- and a stable delivery picture.

## Product Scope
Optima manages care coordination workflows around referral routing, provider follow-up reminders, and training materials that support partner clinics.

## Current Posture
- On track
- No blockers
- Only low-severity watch items
- Clear owner accountability
- High confidence in generated reports and Ask responses

## Documentation Hygiene Pattern
Optima deliberately demonstrates the behavior the application is designed to encourage:
- major conversations are captured in written artifacts,
- weekly updates are timely and specific,
- decisions are made explicit,
- and structured exports agree with the narrative.

## Demo Value
When users ask why Optima is healthy, the application should be able to answer with confidence and provide corroborating evidence from weeklys, transcript, email, and ADO.
